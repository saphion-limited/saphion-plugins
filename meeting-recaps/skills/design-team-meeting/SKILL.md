# Design team meeting

Minutes of a design team or technical coordination meeting at any RIBA stage. The sections follow RIBA's design team meeting agenda and the
Plan of Work 2020 (brief, responsibility, information requirements, design programme, change control), and record the design risk
discussion CDM 2015 expects of designers and the principal designer.

## Sections, in this order

Leave out any section the meeting said nothing about.

1. **Previous actions and matters arising.**
2. **Brief and changes to it.** Any change to the client's requirements, who raised it and whether it was accepted.
3. **Design progress.** By discipline as raised (architecture, structure, MEP, landscape, others): what was reported done and what is next.
4. **Coordination.** Clashes and interfaces between disciplines and what was agreed to resolve each.
5. **Information.** Information required, from whom and by when (only if a date was said); information released or due under the information
   release schedule; design responsibility questions.
6. **Design risks and CDM.** Each hazard or risk identified in the design, what was agreed to eliminate or reduce it, and what goes to the
   principal designer, the pre-construction information or the construction phase plan. A risk is never recorded as closed unless the meeting
   closed it.
7. **Programme and approvals.** The design programme; planning, building control, Building Safety Regulator and client approvals.
8. **Cost and value.** Cost plan points, value engineering options and what was decided about each.
9. **Change control.** Changes proposed and their status.
10. **To issue formally in writing.** See below.
11. **Next meeting**, if said.

## To issue formally in writing

Minutes are not an instruction, a notice or a confirmation of a change in scope or fee. List here every point from the meeting that sounds as
if it may need one, one line each, saying what it is: a brief or scope change, a fee change, a programme change, an instruction, an early
warning or delay. Open the section with the line: *These minutes are not a notice, an instruction or a change confirmation. Each item below
may need to be issued formally in writing.* This list suggests; it never decides whether something is a valid notice, instruction or change.

## How it reads

- A design decision records what was chosen, by whom if said, and why: the option, the reason, and any condition attached.
- Facts, not commentary. Where disciplines disagreed, record each position, attributed to the discipline or person, and that it was not
  agreed.
- Short bullets under each heading; numbered lists for options compared; bold a changed requirement or an agreed dimension or figure.
- An action is a commitment someone made or the meeting agreed, starting with a verb; its owner only if a person was named, its date only if a
  time was said.

## Leave out

Small talk and sound checks, side conversations, design ideas floated and not taken forward unless the meeting asked for them to be
explored, anything not said in the meeting. When Saphion gives the meeting's facts (title, date, times, who joined), they are shown above the
minutes: never write your own attendance list.

## Example outline

```
Brief and changes to it
The client confirmed two additional meeting rooms on level 2. Accepted, subject to the fee change below.
Coordination
The MEP riser clashes with the stair core opening. Agreed: the structural engineer checks the opening size.
Design risks and CDM
Roof plant access: a permanent edge protection option was agreed in place of temporary measures; to be passed to the principal designer.
Programme and approvals
Planning condition 7 (materials) must be discharged before the frame starts.
To issue formally in writing
These minutes are not a notice, an instruction or a change confirmation. …
- Two additional meeting rooms: brief change needing scope and fee confirmation.
```
