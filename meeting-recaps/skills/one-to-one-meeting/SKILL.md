# One-to-one

A manager's regular check-in with one team member. The recap is for the two of them: what each said they are working on, what is in the way,
what feedback went each way, how the person is developing and what each agreed to do before next time. It is a working record, not a file
note about the person, so it holds only what helps the next conversation.

## Sections, in this order

Leave out any section the meeting said nothing about.

1. **How things are.** Only what the team member chose to share about how they are getting on. Write nothing they did not offer; never
   interpret mood or wellbeing from tone.
2. **Updates and priorities.** What they are working on and what comes first, with anything the manager asked them to put first.
3. **Blockers and support needed.** What is in the way and what help was asked for or offered. A blocker that depends on another person's or
   team's work is written as the work that is held up, not as a remark about the person.
4. **Feedback, both ways.** Feedback the manager gave and feedback the team member gave the manager, each said as specifically as it was said.
5. **Development.** Skills, goals, training, opportunities or career conversations, and what was agreed about them.
6. **Agreed actions for both.** One section holding every action either person agreed to, the manager's as much as the team member's. It has
   no text of its own, only the actions.
7. **To pick up next time.** Topics put off, questions left open and anything either person said they wanted to come back to.

## Personal matters

Leave out health or medical detail, family or personal circumstances, and remarks about colleagues. Health information is special category
data under UK GDPR and does not belong in a meeting recap. Where something personal came up, write only: *A personal matter was
discussed.* and, separately, any support that was agreed, said as the support and never the reason ("time off on Friday was agreed").

## If it became a formal matter

If the conversation turned into a disciplinary, grievance or capability matter (a warning, an allegation, a complaint, a performance
procedure), open the recap with the line: *This is not the record of a formal disciplinary, grievance or capability meeting. Those need their
own notice and written record (Acas Code of Practice).*

## How it reads

- Plain, neutral, in the third person ("The manager asked…", "Sam raised…"), past tense. Specific, not general: what was said, not what
  kind of person said it.
- Short. A line or two for a quick update, more only for a real discussion. Bold a changed priority or an agreed date.
- A decision is what the two settled, in one line, with its reason when given. Where they disagreed and nothing was settled, say so and carry
  it to *To pick up next time*.
- An action is a commitment someone made or both agreed, starting with a verb; its owner only if a person was named, its date only if a time
  was said.

## Leave out

Small talk and sound checks, remarks about colleagues, opinions of the person's character, and anything not said in the meeting. Anything
said to be off the record, privileged legal advice or a private session: note only that it was discussed, never its content. When Saphion
gives the meeting's facts (title, date, times, who joined), they are shown above the recap: never write your own attendance list.

## Example outline

```
Summary
Monthly check-in. The reporting migration is the priority until the end of the month; the team member asked for access to the finance
data and a day of training.

How things are
Sam said the workload was manageable now the audit had finished.
Updates and priorities
- The reporting migration is **first**; the dashboard rework moves to December.
Blockers and support needed
- Access to the finance data is held up awaiting approval.
Feedback, both ways
- The manager asked for earlier warning when a date is at risk.
- Sam asked for clearer briefs when work is handed over.
Development
Sam wants to lead a migration; the manager agreed to put them forward for the next one.
Agreed actions for both
(the actions, listed by Saphion)
To pick up next time
- Whether the training day can be booked before January.
```
