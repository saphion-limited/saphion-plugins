# Sales call

A record of a discovery call, demo or sales meeting with a prospect. It keeps what the prospect said close to their words, because the next
conversation depends on what they actually said they needed, not on what we hoped to hear. It states only facts from the call, and says
plainly where something was not raised.

## Sections, in this order

Write every section. Where the call did not raise it, write **Not discussed**.

1. **Who they are.** The prospect's organisation, their role and what the organisation does, only as said.
2. **Their situation and problem, in their words.** What they are doing today, what is not working and what it costs them, using their own
   phrases where clarity allows.
3. **What success looks like.** What they said they want to be true afterwards, and how they would tell.
4. **Budget.** As said: a figure, a range, a cap or "no budget set". Never estimated, inferred or guessed.
5. **Who decides and how.** The people involved in the decision, their roles, and the process they described (approvals, procurement,
   trials), as said.
6. **Timing and what is driving it.** When they want a decision or a start, and the reason (a deadline, a renewal, an event), as said.
7. **Competitors or alternatives mentioned.** Other suppliers, tools or the option of doing nothing, as the prospect named them.
8. **Objections and how they were answered.** Each concern the prospect raised, one line each, and what was said in reply. Where it was
   not answered, say so and keep it open.
9. **Next step.** What was agreed to happen next, who does it and its date. If no step was agreed, write **No next step agreed**.
10. **Actions.** One section holding every action agreed on the call, ours and theirs. It has no text of its own, only the actions.

## How it reads

- Factual and neutral: what the prospect said, not what we think of them. Short bullets, one point each.
- Attribute a point to the person who said it when the prospect had more than one person on the call.
- Where the prospect and our side disagreed, record both positions and that it was not settled.
- Bold a figure, a date or the next step. No named sales method or framework (no BANT, MEDDPICC or similar labels).
- An action is a commitment someone made or the call agreed, starting with a verb; its owner only if a person was named, its date only if a
  time was said.

## Leave out

Deal scores, a likelihood of closing or a forecast category; opinions of the prospect or their company; our own pricing tactics, discount
limits and negotiating position; small talk and sound checks; and anything not said in the meeting. Anything said to be off the record,
privileged legal advice or a private session: note only that it was discussed, never its content. When Saphion gives the meeting's facts
(title, date, times, who joined), they are shown above the record: never write your own attendance list.

## Example outline

```
Who they are
Priya Shah, operations lead at a regional logistics firm with three depots.
Their situation and problem, in their words
"We rekey every delivery note into the finance system." About two days a week across the depots.
What success looks like
Delivery notes reach finance without rekeying.
Budget
Not discussed
Who decides and how
Priya recommends; the finance director signs off anything over **£10k**.
Timing and what is driving it
Wants a decision before the year-end audit in January.
Competitors or alternatives mentioned
Looked at a spreadsheet macro; has not tried it.
Objections and how they were answered
- Concern about training staff across three sites: a recorded walkthrough was offered.
Next step
Demo for the finance director on **12 November**.
```
